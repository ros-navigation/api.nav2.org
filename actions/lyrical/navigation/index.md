# Navigation Actions - Lyrical

This page lists all navigation actions available in Nav2 for the lyrical distribution.

## Available Actions (4)


### [NavigateToPose](/actions/lyrical/navigatetopose.html)

**Package:** `nav2_msgs`

Navigate robot to a specific pose with obstacle avoidance and recovery behaviors

---

### [FollowWaypoints](/actions/lyrical/followwaypoints.html)

**Package:** `nav2_msgs`

Navigate robot through a series of waypoints with optional task execution at each point

---

### [FollowGPSWaypoints](/actions/lyrical/followgpswaypoints.html)

**Package:** `nav2_msgs`

Navigate robot through GPS-based waypoints for outdoor navigation

---

### [NavigateThroughPoses](/actions/lyrical/navigatethroughposes.html)

**Package:** `nav2_msgs`

Navigate robot through a sequence of poses in order
